Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information this website handles and what happens when you use it. The website is an independent personal-brand site about Mohammad Al Mamun, publicly known as Banglar Krrish.

1. Information we collect directly

The website itself does not require an account, does not collect personal data in a database, and does not use analytics, advertising trackers or marketing cookies.

  • Language preference: when you choose English or Bengali, the choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it. It stays on your device and is never sent to us.
  • Contact form: the form does not send your message to a server. When you press the button, your email application opens with your name, email address, subject and message filled in. The message is sent only if you press send in your own email application or webmail, and it then reaches the recipient and your email provider.

2. Embedded YouTube videos

Videos on this site are hosted by YouTube. The site uses the privacy-enhanced domain youtube-nocookie.com for players.

  • Thumbnail images are requested from YouTube's image servers (i.ytimg.com) when a video card comes near your screen. Your browser therefore contacts YouTube, which may log standard request information such as your IP address.
  • The YouTube player and its script load only after you press play on a video.
  • When you play a video, YouTube may set cookies or process data under its own terms. See the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Fonts and other resources

The Inter and Hind Siliguri fonts are downloaded from this website's own files, not from a third-party font service. The site does not load advertising, social-media widgets or analytics scripts.

4. Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard web-server request information, such as IP addresses and browser details, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

5. Links to other websites

Press cards and biography sources link to news websites and YouTube. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

6. Your choices

  • You can use the website without filling in the contact form.
  • You can clear the saved language choice through your browser settings.
  • You can block third-party content in your browser, though some videos may then not play.
  • To ask about information you have sent through the contact form, reply to your own email thread or use the contact form.

7. Children

The website is not designed to collect information from children. Please do not send personal information through the contact form if you are under 18 without a parent or guardian's involvement.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact form.
