Biography

The story of Mohammad Al Mamun, known as Banglar Krrish. It is based on public media reports and his own statements, with links to each source.

Details are reported information unless stated otherwise. Where sources differ, we say so. No awards, qualifications or affiliations are claimed.

A note on the name

'Krrish' is a fictional character from a Bollywood film franchise. The name 'Banglar Krrish' is a public nickname used in media coverage. This website is not connected to the film's producers.

Early background

Mohammad Al Mamun is from Banbaria (Paikpara) village in Sirajganj Sadar upazila, Bangladesh. Reports describe a long-standing interest in fitness, somersaults and acrobatics. He is reported to have taught himself from online videos, without a formal academy or professional trainer.

Read the original report (RTV Online) ↗

Everyday work

Reports describe him as a pipe-fitting worker who also works with stainless steel and iron. His stunt practice happens around his working hours, and he is reported to practise before or after work. His job is how he supports himself; the stunts are a personal passion.

Read the original report (Talash BD) ↗

Inspiration and costume creation

Inspired by the fictional Bollywood superhero Krrish, he first posted stunt videos in ordinary clothes and received little response. Reports say he could not afford a costume, so he adapted an old black burqa belonging to his elder sister into a black costume and mask. This detail comes from media reports and his own account.

Read the original report (Barta Bazar) ↗

Physical practice and performance

Reports describe about eight to ten years of training and parkour practice. Some interviews give different figures, such as six to seven years. His videos show jumps, somersaults and leaps in public places. He has also said he has been injured while practising. These stunts are dangerous, and we do not encourage anyone to copy them.

Read the original report (Bangla Edition) ↗

Public attention and media coverage

His videos spread on Facebook and YouTube, and several outlets covered his story in October 2026. He has said he hopes to find opportunities in film and to represent Bangladesh through his stunts. The sources below are independent reports.
